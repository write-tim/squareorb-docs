Square Orb Gallery gives you the flexibility to manage styles globally or per gallery[cite: 3].

- **Master Override Switch:** Located in the Global Defaults tab. When enabled, this bypasses all individual block settings and forces every gallery on your site to inherit the centralized styles.

- **Clear Canvas Overrides:** If the Master Override Switch is disabled, individual blocks can retain their own settings. To restore a specific gallery block to the site-wide defaults, click the **Clear Canvas Overrides** button in the block's sidebar.

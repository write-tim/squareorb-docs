To enable the Adobe Lightroom CC integration, follow these steps via the Adobe Developer Console.

<Steps>

1. **Set up an Adobe Project:**
    - Navigate to the [Adobe Developer Console](https://developer.adobe.com/console/) and sign in with your Adobe ID.
    
    - Click **Create new project**.

2. **Add an API:**
    - Click **Add to project** and select **API**.
    
    - Select **Lightroom API** (or the equivalent Creative Cloud API) and click **Next**.
    
    - Select **OAuth 2.0 Web** as the authentication method.
    
    - Enter your **Redirect URI** exactly as displayed on the **Square Orb > Authentication** tab in your WordPress dashboard (e.g., `https://yourdomain.com/wp-admin/admin.php?page=square-orb`).
    
    - Save the configuration.

3. **Retrieve Credentials:**
    - Once the API is added, you will be presented with your **Client ID** (API Key) and **Client Secret**.
    
    - Copy these values securely.
    
    - Return to your WordPress dashboard and enter these credentials into the Adobe Lightroom section under **Square Orb > Authentication**.
    
    - Click **Save Adobe Credentials**, then click **Connect to Lightroom** to initiate the OAuth handshake.

</Steps>

*Note: The Adobe integration currently authenticates your Creative Cloud account via OAuth 2.0. Direct cloud photo browsing and sideloading for Lightroom CC assets is scheduled for the upcoming v1.2 release.*

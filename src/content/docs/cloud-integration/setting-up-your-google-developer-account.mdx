This is required if you want to import photos from Google Photos or use the experimental AI tagging features.

<Steps>

1. **Access the Console:** Visit the [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com/) and sign in with your Google account.

2. **Create a New Project:**
    - Click the project dropdown at the top of the page.
    
    - Click **New Project**.
    
    - Give it a name (e.g., "Aura Gallery Site") and click **Create**.

3. **Enable APIs:**
    - Once inside your new project, navigate to **APIs & Services > Library**.
    
    - Search for the specific services you need (e.g., "Google Photos Picker API" or "Cloud Vision API").
    
    - Click on the API and select **Enable**.

4. **Configure Consent:**
    - Go to **OAuth consent screen** in the sidebar.
    
    - Choose **External** and fill in your contact information. This tells Google who owns the "application" (your website).

    </Steps>

This is required if you want to import your photos directly from Adobe Lightroom CC.

<Steps>

1. **Access the Console:** Visit the [Adobe Developer Console](https://developer.adobe.com/console/) and sign in with your Adobe ID.

2. **Create a Project:**
    - Click **Create new project** from the quick start menu. This creates a container for your API credentials.

3. **Add an API:**
    - Inside your project, click **Add API**.
    
    - Select the **Lightroom API** (or the relevant Creative Cloud API) from the list.
    
    - Choose **OAuth 2.0 Web** as the authentication method.
    
    - Enter your **Redirect URI** exactly as shown in **Square Orb > Authentication** in your WordPress dashboard (e.g., `https://yourdomain.com/wp-admin/admin.php?page=square-orb`).

4. **Save Credentials:**
    - Once the API is configured, the console generates your **Client ID** (API Key) and **Client Secret**. Copy and paste these into the **Square Orb > Authentication** tab in WordPress, click **Save Adobe Credentials**, and then click **Connect to Lightroom**.

</Steps>

*Note: The Adobe integration currently authenticates your Creative Cloud account via OAuth 2.0. Direct cloud photo browsing and sideloading for Lightroom CC assets is scheduled for the upcoming v1.2 release.*

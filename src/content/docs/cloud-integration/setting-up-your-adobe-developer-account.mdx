This is required if you want to import your photos directly from Adobe Lightroom CC.

<Steps>

1. **Access the Console:** Visit the [Adobe Developer Console](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://developer.adobe.com/console/) and sign in with your Adobe ID.

2. **Create a Project:**
    - Click **Create new project** from the quick start menu. This creates a container for your API credentials.

3. **Add an API:**
    - Inside your project, click **Add API**.
    
    - Select the **Lightroom API** (or the relevant Creative Cloud API) from the list.
    
    - Choose **OAuth 2.0** as the authentication method. This ensures that your site can securely "log in" to your Adobe account without storing your actual password.

4. **Save Credentials:**
    - Once the API is added, the console will generate your **Client ID** and **Client Secret**. Keep these in a safe place, as you will need to copy and paste them into the Aura Gallery settings page in WordPress.

</Steps>

- **Google OAuth:** Ensure you have added the correct **Authorized Redirect URI** from the plugin to the Google Cloud Console.

- **API Keys:** Keep your secrets private. Never share your Client Secret or Vision API Key publicly.

- **Verification:** After entering credentials in the plugin, always click "Save" and attempt the "Connect" handshake from the Aura Gallery settings tab to confirm everything is configured correctly.

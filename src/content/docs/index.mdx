## Plugin Features

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="Adobe Lightroom Classic Sync" icon="laptop">
		Seamlessly connect your local editing environment to your live galleries. Sync collections and color-graded exports directly to your website.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Advanced EXIF Extraction" icon="magnifier">
		Automatically pull and display detailed technical metadata alongside your images, from camera configurations to complex exposure bracketing settings.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Responsive Gallery Layouts" icon="rocket">
		Build touch-friendly masonry grids, justified rows, and interactive carousels that adapt perfectly to any screen size.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Image Protection" icon="setting">
		Safeguard your high-resolution files using dynamic watermarking and right-click deterrents without sacrificing visual quality.
	</Card>

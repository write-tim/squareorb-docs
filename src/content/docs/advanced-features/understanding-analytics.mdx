Square Orb Gallery anonymously tracks silent telemetry on user engagement.

- Visit the **Analytics** tab to view your top-performing assets.

- The dashboard graphs **Total Views** and **Total Dwell Time** (in seconds), allowing you to see exactly which images or videos capture the most attention.

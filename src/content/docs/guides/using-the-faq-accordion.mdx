Our documentation includes a custom, reusable `<FAQ>` component that allows you to create interactive, collapsible accordion menus. This is perfect for organizing long troubleshooting pages or grouping common billing questions.

## How to Add an FAQ

To use the FAQ component in your article, you must first import it at the top of your document, and then wrap your content in the `<FAQ>` tags.

### 1. Import the Component

Add this exact line to the very top of your article:

`import FAQ from '../../../components/FAQ.astro';`

### 2. Write the Tags

Provide the question inside the `title=""` attribute, and place the answer between the opening and closing tags. You can use standard Markdown (like bolding, lists, and links) inside the answer!

<FAQ title="What is your refund policy?">
We offer a full refund within the first **30 days** of your subscription. 

To request a refund, please contact Support.
</FAQ>

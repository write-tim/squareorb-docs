Square Orb provides multi-layered asset protection tools designed to safeguard creative assets against casual downloading, unauthorized scraping, and content theft.

---

## 1. Right-Click Protection

When enabled, Square Orb actively blocks context menu interactions across your galleries and lightbox presentations.

* **Scope of Protection:** Applies directly to gallery container items (`.sorb-protected`) and the full-screen lightbox overlay (`.sorb-lightbox-protected`).
* **Interaction Prevention:** Disables native right-click context menus (`Save Image As...`, `Copy Image Link`) and prevents users from dragging images off the page.
* **Granular Control:** Can be enabled globally on the **Global** tab (`tab=sitewide`) or toggled individually on each **Square Orb Canvas** block.
* *Security Note:* While right-click protection effectively deters casual website visitors, it is a client-side deterrent. Determined users with browser inspection tools can still access network assets. For absolute asset security, pair right-click protection with non-destructive watermarking.

---

## 2. Non-Destructive PNG Watermarking Engine

Square Orb features a server-side watermarking engine built to protect your high-resolution images without degrading your original files.

* **Non-Destructive Stamping:** When a watermark is applied, Square Orb stamps only the web-generated intermediate sizes (`sorb-pro-highres`, `large`, `medium_large`). Your original, untouched master file is preserved in your uploads directory.
* **Transparent PNG Support:** Stamping preserves alpha channel transparency, ensuring clean visual stamps without opaque rectangular artifacts.
* **Proportional Scaling:** Watermarks automatically scale to 15% of the target image's width (maintaining aspect ratio) with a 3% edge padding.
* **Positioning:** Choose between **Bottom Right** (ideal for discrete logos or copyright signatures) or **Center** (ideal for proofing and stock media previews).

---

## 3. Auto-Watermarking on Upload

Under **Square Orb > Global**:

* Enable **Auto-Watermark Native Uploads** to automatically process and stamp all new images as they are uploaded to the WordPress Media Library.
* Cloud imports through Google Photos are also processed automatically when watermarking is configured.

---

## 4. Media Library Bulk Actions

If you already have images in your Media Library or want to watermark or remove watermarks from specific batches:

1. Navigate to **Media > Library** in your WordPress dashboard.
2. Switch to the **List View** (the icon with rows, top left above the media items).
3. Select the checkboxes next to the images you wish to modify.
4. Open the **Bulk Actions** dropdown menu at the top of the table:
   * Select **Apply Square Orb Watermark** to stamp the selected media.
   * Select **Remove Square Orb Watermark** to regenerate intermediate sizes and remove the watermark cleanly.
5. Click **Apply**. Square Orb will batch-process the images and display a confirmation notice.

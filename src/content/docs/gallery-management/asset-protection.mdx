Square Orb Gallery includes built-in tools to deter unauthorized downloading.

- **Watermark Overlay (PNG):** Upload a transparent PNG to stamp over your images. You can set the position (e.g., bottom right or center). This is non-destructive and preserves your original uploaded files. You can also enable **Auto-Watermark Native Uploads** to apply this automatically to all new uploads.

- **Right-Click Protection:** Disables the browser's native right-click "save image" function on gallery items and within the Lightbox.
